AI engineering

How I put language models and agents into production.

  • Protect the data

    De-identification is part of the integration from the first proof of concept.

  • Control cost and reliability

    Token-usage logging, caching, rate limiting and retries keep the service dependable at 10,000+ documents per day.

  • Make prompts measurable

    Prompt-to-result tracking lets data science trace each result back to its prompt and iterate.

  • Keep people in the loop

    The agentic contract-testing workflow routes flagged failures to a person for review.

What I've built

Four projects from my time at Munich Re. Each opens a walkthrough with a diagram and the full details.

First production OpenAI integration

Owned end to end, from scoping requirements with data scientists to a proof of concept with de-identification, token-usage logging, caching, rate limiting and retries. It became the template for later integrations.

Prompt-to-result tracking lets data science iterate on prompts. The service processed 10,000+ documents per day.

Agentic contract testing

An agentic workflow that keeps service contracts consistent between services. An agent updates contracts when a new vendor is onboarded and catches breakage introduced by refactors, with human-in-the-loop review of flagged failures.

Third-party ordering platform

Delivered a microservices-based ordering application to three clients, integrating three vendors. Multi-step workflows are orchestrated with OSS Conductor across Python and FastAPI services on Kubernetes.

Shipped in two months, accelerating a roadmap planned for a year, and recognized with a Spot Bonus from the SVP of Digital Solutions.

FHIR platform migration

Planned and led the migration of 16 million client records from a legacy FHIR service to a new platform with zero downtime.

In the same role: infrastructure provisioned with Terraform, role-based access control for Azure resources and SQL Server, a Gravitee API gateway with OAuth 2.0, and load-tested scaling to 10x more parallel requests.

Production ownership and compliance
